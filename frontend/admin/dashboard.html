Quick Start

Upload a PDF lesson note to automatically generate MCQ questions and a structured summary. Then review and approve at least 40 questions before publishing for students.

Workflow

1

Upload a PDF under the Upload PDF section — AI will extract text and generate questions + summary automatically.

2

Review generated questions in Questions — edit, delete bad ones, and approve exactly 40.

3

Review and edit the lesson summary under Summaries if needed.

4

Go to Courses and click Publish to make the course live for students.

Upload PDF

Drop your PDF here or click to browse

Maximum file size: 20MB. Text-based PDFs only.

What happens next

  • Text is extracted from the PDF
  • AI generates 50–60 multiple-choice questions
  • AI creates a structured lesson summary
  • You review and approve questions before publishing

Processing typically takes 30–60 seconds depending on PDF length.
